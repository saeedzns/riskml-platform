Logistic regression has the higher ROC-AUC. XGBoost has higher average precision and F1 and a lower
Brier score in this fixed evaluation. Neither model is designated the winner. XGBoost is the
explanation-capable champion artifact used by the API.

The generated evidence also includes explicit confusion-matrix cells and false-positive,
false-negative, and illustrative-cost values at thresholds 0.3, 0.5, and 0.7. The cost calculation is
only a transparent sensitivity illustration; its weights are not validated business economics or a
production policy.

Neither result establishes generalization. No hyperparameter tuning used the held-out test set. The
model is not calibrated post hoc because the dataset is small; Brier score is calibration-sensitive
evidence, not proof of calibration. A production candidate would require nested or out-of-fold
calibration, temporal and external validation, fairness and compliance review, and a validated
decision-cost framework.

Exact machine-readable metrics are generated at `artifacts/metrics-logistic.json` and
`artifacts/metrics-xgboost.json`, logged to MLflow, and flattened for the Power BI presentation.
Generated evidence is deliberately ignored rather than committed as model state.
